## 🌟 Project Summary
This project presents a **Zomato Restaurant Performance Analysis Dashboard** built using **Tableau and Power BI**. The analysis provides comprehensive insights into restaurant operations, customer behavior, geographical distribution, and key business metrics to drive data-informed decision-making for restaurant stakeholders and business analysts.

The project includes:
- **SQL Database**: Structured queries for data extraction and transformation
- **Excel Dashboard**: Intermediate data processing and analysis
- **Tableau Dashboard**: Interactive visualization of restaurant metrics
- **Power BI Dashboard**: Advanced business intelligence and KPI tracking

## 🧠 Key Insights
🔹 **Geographic Concentration** - Restaurant presence and orders are concentrated in major metropolitan areas  
🔹 **Rating Paradox** - Higher-priced restaurants don't always have higher ratings; quality and consistency matter more  
🔹 **Order Volume Drivers** - Delivery speed, restaurant visibility, and customer reviews are key order volume drivers  
🔹 **Cuisine Preferences** - Local cuisine types vary significantly by region; regional preferences are strong  
🔹 **Peak Hours** - Lunch (12-2 PM) and dinner (7-9 PM) are peak ordering periods  
🔹 **Customer Reviews Impact** - Restaurants with more reviews tend to have higher engagement and orders  
🔹 **Price Sensitivity** - Mid-range restaurants (₹300-₹500 average cost) show highest order frequency  
🔹 **Seasonal Trends** - Weekend orders are significantly higher than weekday orders  
🔹 **Multi-Item Orders** - Restaurants offering variety see higher order values and frequency  
🔹 **New Restaurant Performance** - New restaurants (< 6 months) show different rating patterns than established ones
